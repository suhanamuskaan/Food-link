# 🍲 Food-Link

> Bridging the gap between food surplus and food security by connecting local donors with NGOs in real time.

---

## 🌟 About The Project

**Food-Link** is a platform designed to combat food waste and hunger. Every day, restaurants, event organizers, grocery stores, and households throw away surplus food, while nearby shelters and NGOs struggle to secure daily meals for those in need. Food-Link acts as a seamless digital bridge, allowing donors to list surplus food quickly and enabling verified NGOs to claim and distribute it before it spoils.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **For Donors (Restaurants, Event Hosts, Individuals):**
  * **Quick Listing:** Post surplus food details (quantity, type, pickup window, and dietary info) in seconds.
  * **Status Tracking:** Monitor whether your donation has been claimed, picked up, or successfully delivered.
  * **Impact Metrics:** View a summary of meals shared and waste reduced.
* **For NGOs & Shelters:**
  * **Real-Time Alerts:** Get notified instantly when fresh food becomes available nearby.
  * **Claim & Coordinate:** Secure available food donations with a single click and coordinate pickup logistics.
  * **Directory & Trust:** Verified profiles to ensure safety and transparency.
* **General Features:**
  * **Geolocation Mapping:** Find nearby food donors and recipient organizations visually.
  * **Secure Authentication:** Role-based access control for Donors, NGOs, and Admin users.
